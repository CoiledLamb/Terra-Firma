# Fresh-air jam — 2026-08-18

Free-form jam session, no docket. User: "why don't we have a breath of fresh air and just jam for
a bit? on anything." Nothing here is committed work; the top 5 below are EARMARKED by the user
(parked in TODO.md's Pending design budget), the rest is recorded for provenance only.

## User's top 5 (ranked, user's own list 2026-08-18)

1. **Accretion** — mutation. Walking over rubble tiles slowly builds temporary AV plating that
   sheds when you take a big hit. CLAUDE PROPOSAL, user-earmarked before the ranking ("accretion
   I would earmark").
2. **Unwritten armor** — USER CONCEPT (their wording: "unwritten armor that hardens when bled
   on"). Descendant of two jam threads: the mudblood/Calcifying defect (blood mineralizing where
   it clots) and the unfinished cuirass (armor carved as an incomplete statue of the wearer).
   Name and shape are the user's.
3. **Grinder cell** — energy cell recharged by feeding it rubble. Rides the standard energy-cell
   interface. CLAUDE PROPOSAL.
4. **Calcifying / Geode** — the mineralization pair. Calcifying (defect): bleeds stack a small
   move-speed penalty until healed, sealed wounds grant a sliver of AV. Geode (mutation): heavy
   hits convert some damage into a brittle state, AV up / DV down. CLAUDE PROPOSALS. Naming note:
   the user independently invented the same defect under the name "mudblood"; Claude flagged that
   the word is also the Harry Potter blood-purity slur and many players will read the reference
   first. Naming is the user's domain and stays OPEN.
5. **Mortar flask** — thrown pot of wet stone that sets into a real wall object where it lands.
   Carve-and-fill counterpart to the mod's demolition verbs. CLAUDE PROPOSAL.
