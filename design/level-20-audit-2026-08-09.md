# Level-20 scaling audit (2026-08-09)

Context: mutants can rapid-advance mutations past the rank cap (user directive this
sitting: "mutants can rapidly advance a mutation up 4 levels, and those levels don't
count for the cap, so they can reach level 20 even on physical mutations"). Every live
scaling formula audited to L20. Scope per user: current Fissure pulse kit, Gorgon's
Gaze, Earthen Barrage, Stoneshaper, Calcify — Geomancy, Lithovore, and retired Fissure
mechanics excluded. Status: CLAUDE ANALYSIS; flags need rulings, nothing changed.

Method: formulas evaluated directly; penetration claims measured against the ported
decomp pen roll (Stat.cs:160, same sim as the spec's v5 section). Wall AVs verified
from game data: sandstone 8, shale 10, limestone 15, marble 25, granite 40.

## Fissure (pulse kit)

| Dial | L10 | L15 | L20 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pulse dice (staircase) | 5d4/3d4/2d4 (25) | 6d4/5d4/3d4 (35) | 7d4/6d4/5d4 (45) | by design — the staircase is deliberately uncapped (v5 ruling); paper grows ~2/level |
| Stun rounds | 3 | 4 (from L13) | 5 (from L17) | staircase design; DC cap keeps it saveable |
| Stun save DC | 20 (cap) | 20 | 20 | capped by ruling — boosts buy rounds, never DC |
| Push force | 12000 | 17000 | 22000 | monotone, thunderdome sort intended |
| Creature PV | 4+Str | 4+Str | 4+Str | level-free by ruling |
| Ring dials (gaps/wall share/etc.) | band 4 | frozen | frozen | GetBand caps at 4; all fabric dials freeze at L10 values |
| **Carve PV 6+3L** | **36** | **51** | **66** | **FLAG 1 — see below** |

**FLAG 1 — the granite boundary collapses.** Measured per-bite outcomes (34%-of-max
per pen, 3+ pens = fell; ring-1 walls take 3 bites per cast):

| | sandstone 8 | limestone 15 | marble 25 | granite 40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L10 (PV 36) | auto-fell | auto-fell | auto-fell (6.9 pens) | 0.81 pens/bite → ~82% max HP per CAST |
| L15 (PV 51) | auto | auto | auto | auto-fell (6.9 pens/bite) |
| L20 (PV 66) | auto | auto | auto | auto (14.6 pens/bite) |

Granite — the "deep hard stone as a real material boundary" of the redoubt draft —
already falls in ~2 casts at L10 under per-pulse bites, and fells per-bite from ~L13.
Note the CLASS boundary is intact at every level: constructed/metal/machine walls
(Hard class) are damage-clamped to 1 HP and never fall, so "the World Engine's bones
don't answer" survives L20. The question is only whether NATURAL granite should yield
to a rapid-advanced earth-shaper. Options: (a) accept — thematically defensible, the
class boundary is the real wall; (b) cap carve PV at 36 (the L10 value) so L10's
material relationships freeze; (c) attenuate bite PV per pulse (the spec's open
low-band governor, applied top-down). AWAITING RULING.

## Earthen Barrage

| Dial | L10 | L15 | L20 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Throw damage | 4d6+1 | 6d6 | 7d6+2 | monotone, sane |
| Max charges | 12 | 17 | 22 | sane |
| Stone tier | boulder | boulder | boulder | ≥10 branch holds |
| **Pen bonus Lv/3** | **+3** | **+5** | **+6** | **FLAG 2** |

**FLAG 2 — the pen cut un-cuts itself.** The 2026-08-06 rebalance slowed pens to Lv/3
specifically because pen multiplication vs soft armor produced the L4 electrofuge
one-shots — but the sim (design/eb_damage_sim.py) only ran to L10 (+3). At L20 the
bonus is +6, twice the ceiling that rebalance established. Options: (a) accept — L20
is rarefied; (b) cap at +3 (the L9/L10 value, freezing the rebalance's ceiling); (c)
re-run eb_damage_sim to 20 and dial from measurements. AWAITING RULING.

## Calcify

| Dial | L10 | L15 | L20 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 50 (floor) | 50 | 50 | capped, fine |
| **Walls created 4+L/2** | **9** | **11** | **14** | **FLAG 3** |

**FLAG 3 — the Force Wall anchor drifts.** The count was tuned to hit "exactly 9 at
Lv10 — Force Wall's count at full investment, in permanent stone" (code comment). Past
10 it keeps growing: 14 permanent walls per 50-round cast at L20. Options: (a) accept
— more rampart per cast is a clean boost payoff; (b) cap at 9 to keep the Force Wall
parity as a hard identity. AWAITING RULING.

## Formula-safety sweep (all green)

No division/overflow/format hazards anywhere in scope: all getters are total functions
of Lv; dice strings render correctly at every level (Stoneshaper's float dice count
formats clean); level text renders at any rank since it rides the getters; GetBand's
Min(4,...) cap freezes every band-keyed dial rather than breaking it; Fissure's
GetGeyserBlueprint caps at Omnigeyser3.
