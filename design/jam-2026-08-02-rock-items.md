# Jam note 2026-08-02 — three rock-item concepts

**Status: USER CONCEPTS from a jam session (source: Blue Protocol's Heavy Guardian kit +
broader earthbending survey). All elaborations below the concept lines are CLAUDE PROPOSAL,
UNRULED. Nothing here is scoped, ruled, or beta material — post-1.0 fodder only.**

**The unifying observation (Claude, unruled):** with these three, every catalog rock becomes a
multi-verb object — eat (Lithovore), throw (EB/catalog), grind (mill), plus pull / wear / shoot.
Rocks as the mod's universal currency; your build decides which verbs you live by.

---

## 1. Pulling weapon ("sand grab" / hypertractor-adjacent)

**User concept:** a hypertractor-like pulling weapon built on the sand-vacuum idea
(Blue Protocol Sandgrip: 30-Rage vacuum pull + interrupt + 70% slow).

Claude elaboration, unruled:
- Base-game hypertractor proves the pull plumbing exists in-engine; ride it, don't reinvent.
- Shape fork: activated-beam artifact copy (safe, derivative) vs **heavy melee weapon**
  (maul/gauntlet family — Cudgel-adjacent, so Stonecarver-friendly) with an activated
  cone/line vacuum on cooldown: drag creatures 2-3 cells toward wielder, Strength save for
  big creatures, brief grit slow.
- The twist worth fighting for: **pulls loose objects too** — harvest fallen fissure rocks,
  drag Lithovore dinner, restock the EB reserve from range. Combat item that doubles as a
  gathering tool.
- Emergent play: pull enemies across your own dropped hazards (hexstone, cracked tiles, tar).

## 2. Rock-socket armor

**User concept:** armor that gains temporary AV when rocks are slotted into it.

Claude elaboration, unruled:
- Chassis: energy-cell socket idiom, socket takes rocks.
- Durability: slotted rock is **ablative with visible stages** (intact → cracked → crumbles);
  you wear out your geology and re-slot from the floor. Supply loop with the puller and with
  Fissure itself.
- Identity fork: maximalist = per-rock wear-riders mirroring the eaten-effects table
  (61-entry content pass, park hard) vs sane = AV by rock tier + riders for ~5 marquee rocks,
  framework left open.
- Optional Stonecarver hook: chisel-work shapes a slotted rock to fit (slower cracking) —
  makes the calling the best wearer without touching its stats.

## 3. Gravel shotgun

**User concept:** shotgun that breaks a rock apart and fires it as a gravel blast of pebbles.

Claude elaboration, unruled:
- **It is a handheld stone mill with a barrel — let the ruled mill law govern it for free:**
  rocks the mill grinds chamber fine (pebble spread, tier-scaled dice); rocks the mill
  refuses jam or worse; quarrelquartz remains a memorable decision.
- Design guard: the spread does NOT carry the rock's exotic eaten/thrown payload — grinding
  to gravel destroys identity, same as the mill's powder step. Balance valve + thematically
  consistent.
- Economy: the aggressive consumer of the rock supply; competes with EB and Lithovore for
  feedstock (healthy tension). Gives non-mutants a way into the rock economy.

---

**Claude priority gut-read (unruled):** shotgun = most buildable (mill law + shotgun idiom
exist) · puller = most fun per line of code (one ability, huge emergent surface) · armor =
deepest, scales with per-rock content invested.

Related jam threads not yet captured anywhere: seismic sense (ATLA), voluntary self-
petrification / statue stance (DCSS Statue Form; inverts the shipped stone-mark effect),
rock-to-mud terrain softening (D&D), granite gaol containment (FFXIV), solidifying-sand
fissure rock (Blue Protocol Sandshroud).

# Round 2 (same day) — bending verbs + the earth gearset

## 4. Wall-kick gear piece — USER LEAN: gear, not mutation rider

**Design history (user): the earliest draft of Earthen Barrage was exactly this — summon a
wall in front of you, kick it for damage in a straight line.** Returns as gear, same story
as the hypertractor. USER LEAN 2026-08-02: too strong as a mutation rider; as a gear piece
the combo potential is the point (user was independently thinking Calcify synergy: raise
wall, kick wall). Claude riffs, unruled: "kick any adjacent wall" variant makes corridors
ammo; ruins' metal walls refuse the kick (connection-doctrine limit / metalbending nod);
kicked wall terminates as rubble; feet slot.

## 5. Fissure ground-wave meditation — USER MEDITATION, DECISION GATED ON BETA RECEPTION

User's shape (2026-08-02, not a ruling — "very big potential here, but it really depends on
how it's received in playtesting"): if current Fissure proves too confusing/bloated in the
friends beta —
- trim the rock table hard, to just the thematically strongest options
- potentially CUT LITHOVORE ("sad as it's our main defect, but so it goes")
- reshape Fissure: hurts + displaces nearby enemies, leaves a RING OF ROCKS AND RUBBLE
  around you; maybe the ring has holes; maybe cracks in the floor
- resulting symmetry: **Fissure = Force Bubble analogue, Calcify = Force Wall analogue**
- worst case: the ideation becomes an item (joins the gearset) rather than a mutation change

Claude notes, unruled: a rock ring does NOT move with you (unlike Force Bubble) — it's a
redoubt you raise where you stand, not a mobile shield; holes in the ring + existing crack
mechanic = the gaps are trap-gates, not weaknesses. **Beta-read confound to remember: the 61
rock descriptions are BETA-HELD (generic fallback text), so beta players will find the table
LESS legible than the shipped mod will be — don't over-read tester confusion as table
failure.**

## 6. Catch-and-return — USER: "definitely leaning gloves," tier LEAN low

USER LEAN 2026-08-02: gloves; part of "building out a gearset of earth manipulation items,
which … fits a natural niche within the mod." Tier: user leans LOW. Claude support, unruled:
catching is scaling-neutral (value = what enemies throw, which deepens with depth), so low
tier is safe and maximizes time spent with the verb. Rocks-only catch scope. Reactive
trigger (incoming thrown rock → save → EB-reserve/inventory) over a stance.

**The emerging gearset (unruled sketch):** gloves (catch, low tier) · boots (wall-kick, mid)
· puller weapon (mid-high) · rock-socket armor (scales with rock content) · possible
rubble-ring item if the Fissure meditation lands item-side. Rubble-as-first-class-object
keeps recurring as the connective tissue.

## 5b. Fissure meditation, deepened (USER, same day)

User's diagnosis of current Fissure: "essentially creating a minefield of fun grenades to
play with … good in theory but its identity has kind of been flattened." Ideal identity:
**"creating a rough form of cover and area control for yourself."** Middle ground stays
possible: the ring can still spawn strong rocks, "or even statues or figures as well" —
which pushes it further from Burgeoning toward Force Bubble, "which might be a stronger
identity" (USER LEAN, still gated on beta reception).

Claude riffs, unruled: invert product/byproduct — terrain is the product (ring pieces =
movement/LOS blockers raised NOW), salvage rocks are the *afterlife* of broken/expired ring
pieces, drawn from a trimmed strong-rock list (catalog investment demoted, not deleted;
Lithovore survives on the trimmed list). Heaved stone FIGURES as the rare premium ring
piece: blocks LOS like a wall, reads as the earth surfacing half-formed sculpture (lore
surface = user's), and is Stoneshaper-animatable — the statue ecosystem (Gorgon makes,
Stoneshaper wakes, Stonecarver carves) gains a fourth member: the earth offers. Calcify
guard: Calcify = deliberate architecture, Fissure = catastrophe you stand at the center
of — "rough" (random gaps, random materials, crack-gates under holes) IS the differentiator.

## 5c. Redoubt-Fissure mechanical sketch (USER SHAPE, same day — jam, not ruling)

User: the stone does NOT expire. Ring scales with level in size, strength, and stun
capability. Cast = **a wave of earth radiating out from the user** (possible animation:
stone walls rising from the ground and disappearing as the wave travels) that **pushes
everything away** and **forms the rough ring at the edge where the wave dissipates.**

Claude riffs, unruled: the image is an impact crater — caster as impact point, ring as
ejecta rim; the wave PLOWS material outward, unifying the earlier ground-wave thread with
the redoubt. Creatures that resist the push (size/strength save) stay trapped INSIDE with
the caster — the wave sorts the room; thunderdome as feature, self-balancing (only the
strong get locked in). Fixed gap count + circumference growing with level = the ring gets
proportionally more complete as it scales without ever being airtight. Recast into an
existing ring: the new wave plows the old rubble outward — concentric, reinforced rings
over a long hold. No expiry + Qud's persistent zones = old battle sites stay marked; the
world remembers your fights (Stonecarver-commemoration adjacency). Animation plumbing: the
mod has shipped ephemeral-object work before (ephemeral munitions in the sound pass), so
rising-and-vanishing wall sprites have in-house precedent.
