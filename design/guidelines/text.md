## All AI-produced text is draft until the user rewrites or signs off

- ruled: 2026-07-24
- user, 2026-07-25 (found by the transcript sweep; the tracker line itself has no quote): "this is why all the text in the mod is a draft - needs a human to write it or at least look over and sign off. so increasing text debt is as much of a concern as sprite debt, to me"
- source: `TODO.md:2593`
- applies to: anything a player reads. Descriptions, level text, greetings, the manifest.
- shape: text debt clears at sign-off, not at generation, and weighs the same as sprite debt. Context at the ruling: Claude asserted a wrong basic fact the same session it pitched writing an anchor book.

## Read the game's own books and absorb its tone before proposing lore

- ruled: 2026-07-23, affirmed 2026-09-03 ("reading the lore before proposing things are great")
- user: "i'd like you to read the books and get familiar with the lore and tone of the game as closely as possible before suggesting anything"
- source: transcript sweep, `design/transcript-sweep-2026-09-03.md`
- applies to: any lore, naming, description or dialogue proposal. The constraint sibling is CLAUDE.md's "never invent lore"; this is the positive half, do the reading first.
