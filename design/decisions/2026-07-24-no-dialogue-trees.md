# NPC conversations: no bespoke dialogue trees

- status: live
- ruled: 2026-07-24
- user: "same amount of work, presented differently"
- source: `JOURNAL.md:2594`
- expires with: the creatures workstream

All pack NPCs use the base-game minor-NPC idiom: one flavorful greeting line plus the standard
trade option ("Live and drink" precedent). The writing budget goes into the greeting lines and
look-descriptions, which reads more in-universe than scripted trees. Trade and the water ritual
are engine parts, not conversation nodes. Doctrine rulings such as consent flavor the greetings
and descriptions rather than gating a conversation layer.
