## Transition (ruled the same sitting; user: "that sounds fine")

The base keeps everything it has shipped through the TF2 beta. The TF2 beta ships only
never-shipped content, additively. The strip happens once, at TF2 1.0, announced to subscribers
and in the patch notes. "Terra Firma 2" is the user's phrasing at the ruling; confirm as the
final name at the workshop-names call.

Existing-save compatibility remains owed before the strip. The earlier calling-move test is
superseded by the content placement below: Stonecarver stays in TF1. Test the actual moved
content and shared-provider transitions instead.

## Dependency and shared support

- closed: 2026-09-27
- status: in effect; implementation and verification remain open
- supersedes: the 2026-09-12 shared-content arrangement (one source bundled into both mods,
  one provider loaded). TF2 content creates and calls TF1 mutations throughout, so a
  standalone TF2 would have carried nearly all of TF1.
- expires with: replacement of the two-mod packaging arrangement

TF2 requires TF1 through its manifest `Dependencies`. Shared support lives once, in TF1; no
bundled copies, no `Directories`/`Exclusions` provider selection. Players who want TF2
without the mutations turn off the existing per-mutation pool options.

TF1 standalone fixes: the Stonecarver starting kit drops `Cleo_TerraFirma_MiningGear`. TF2
adds the Quarriers reputation by a Stonecarver subtype merge, and the boar vessel's wild-spawn
tags by `Load="Merge"` onto a TF1 `Cleo_TerraFirma_BaseBoarVessel` that carries none.

Verify TF1 alone and TF1 with TF2, plus save transitions (types resolve by full name, so
code moving between the two assemblies keeps loading; fields serialized through
`BinaryFormatter` record the assembly name and need checking). Engine facts behind this:
`XRL.ModInfo.InitializeFiles`/`ResolveDependencies`, `XRL.SubtypeEntry.MergeWith`,
`XRL.World.Loaders.ObjectBlueprintLoader` (an unmarked duplicate name replaces silently).
