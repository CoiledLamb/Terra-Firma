The base grammar Every base mutation follows the same two-part formula. This is what makes the section automatable.

Flavor line — GetDescription

  • One declarative sentence, second person, present tense. "You emit a ray of flame." · "You animate…" · "Your wounds heal very quickly."
  • "You/Your" opener is near-universal. Rare sanctioned variants: subject-first ("Horns jut out of your head.") and the adverbial opener ("Through sheer force of will, you perform uncanny physical feats." — WillForce).
  • 4–8 words is the home register; the long tail is earned by spectacle (SlogGlands, 24 words of sphincter-choked bilge hose).
  • No numbers, no stats — for leveled mutations, mechanics live entirely in level text. Defects and non-leveled mutations instead append precise mechanic lines after a blank line (Carnivorous: "You eat meat exclusively.\n\nYou get no satiation from…").
  • Punctuation is bare: zero em-dashes in 131 mutations, one semicolon, parentheses only for glosses like "(MA)".

Level text — GetLevelText

  • One fact per line. Mechanism sentence(s) first, then stat lines, then Cooldown last, then a reputation line if any.
  • Mechanism sentences are terse: subjectless third person ("Creates 9 contiguous squares of immobile forcefield.") or "You may / You can…" — never imperative.
  • Stat labels from a fixed vocabulary: Range · Area · Damage increment · Duration · Cone length/angle · Cooldown: N rounds.
  • {{rules|…}} wraps scaling values; flat constants usually go bare ("Cooldown: 10 rounds" · SunderMind's "Range: sight" is unwrapped).
  • No editorializing. The text states what happens; it never explains design intent or adds asides.

Gorgon's Gaze closest to house voice

Terra Firma

"Your unblinking gaze stops creatures in their tracks."

"Taking any action other than passing the turn will break your gaze." … "Targets that escape after {{rules|4}} or more failures are left partially petrified: −20 move speed, −10 Quickness, +2 AV for 500 rounds" "Range: {{rules|sight}}" "Cooldown: {{rules|80}} rounds"

Base touchstone — Sunder Mind

"You sunder the mind of an enemy, leaving them reeling in pain."

"Taking any action other than passing the turn will break the connection." "Range: sight" "Cooldown: 175 rounds"

  • ✓Deliberately mirrors Sunder Mind's structure line-for-line, and it shows — this is the model the rest can follow.
  • △The stone-mark line packs four stats and a duration into one line; base grammar wants it split (one fact per line).
  • △"Range: {{rules|sight}}" — Sunder Mind leaves "sight" unwrapped; rules-wrap is for scaling numbers.
Suggested fix — DRAFT (mechanical only; everything else stands)

"Targets that escape after {{rules|4}} or more failures are left partially petrified for 500 rounds. Partial petrification: −20 move speed, −10 Quickness, +2 AV Range: sight Cooldown: 80 rounds"

The stone-mark line split in two (effect + its stats); "sight" and the flat cooldown unwrapped per base convention (constants go bare, scaling values wrap).

Earthen Barrage worked 2026-07-26 — upper ladder accepted as drafted ("it's fine"); only the parked whorl-DV redraft remains

Terra Firma

"You form hefty rocks suitable for throwing."

"Penetration bonus: {{rules|+N}} (attuned stones forgo it; resonance pierces by matching, not force)" "While active, a sculpted stone occupies your thrown weapon slot and is replaced from your reserve when thrown." "…granting {{rules|+2}}/{{rules|+4}} DV in place of AV"

Base touchstone — Electrical Generation

"You accrue electrical charge that you can use and discharge to deal damage."

"Maximum charge: {{C|N}}" "Accrue base {{C|N}} charge per turn" "EMP causes involuntary discharge (difficulty 18 Willpower save)"

  • ✓Flavor line and stat labels ("Damage increment:") are on-formula; the resource-economy structure has a clean base model in Electrical Generation.
  • ✗The parenthetical "resonance pierces by matching, not force" is design rationale inside rules text — no base precedent (1 semicolon in the whole base corpus; this text has one in one line).
  • ✗Several compound lines carry two or three facts each; base splits them. The whorl/deflect block reads as prose where base would ladder stats.
Suggested fix — DRAFT (full level-text redraft; flavor line stands)

"You sculpt a stone every {{rules|N}} rounds, up to a reserve of {{rules|N}}. Sculpted stones occupy your thrown weapon slot. Stones not held as a thrown weapon crumble to dust after 50-100 rounds. Damage increment: {{rules|N}} Penetration bonus: {{rules|+N}} Daze chance on penetrating hit: {{rules|N}}% Stones in reserve whorl about you and deflect physical projectiles. Lasers and other energy attacks pass through the whorl. Deflect chance: 3% per stone in reserve, +1% per mutation level +1 AV while 3 or more stones are in reserve +2 AV while 9 or more stones are in reserve"

At level 10+, the resonance block — REVISED ×2 at the user sitting, 2026-07-26: "You may attune your stones to {{C|resonance}}. Resonant stones' penetration value is equal to the defender's armor value. While attuned, your whorl deflects light instead of matter and grants +2 or +4 DV in place of AV."

Sitting record: retune line CUT (user — QoL goes undocumented, base idiom). Reserve clause CUT from the slot line (user — reads as base behavior; nuance recorded: EB actually refills from the reserve, not the bag, but the sculpt line already implies it). Vibro phrasing RECAST on recon (user asked for precedent): base explains the mechanic in exactly one settled sentence — "When charged, this weapon's penetration value is equal to the defender's armor value." (RulesDescription on Vibro Dagger + Vibro Blade; the geomagnetic disc, our thrown model, documents nothing) — so the line states the rule and drops the word "vibro"; the cost clause ("in place of {{rules|+N}} penetration bonus") was then CUT too (user) — the forgone bonus goes undocumented, which is in fact the deeper precedent: the vibro dagger/blade rules text names no trade-off either, and the geomagnetic disc says nothing at all. "While attuned, your whorl…" stands on Spinnerets' state-conditional shape. The parenthetical aside stays gone. Flat constants unwrapped (user RULED "do whatever is most base game accurate there"): 50-100 lifetime, deflect 3%/+1%, AV +1/+2, DV +2/+4 all go bare per the Electrical Generation formula-line pattern; computed values (sculpt rate, reserve, damage, pen, daze chance) keep their wraps. BEHAVIOR FLAG 2026-07-26: the DV-in-place-of-AV line is accurate to current code, but the user flagged the behavior itself for a fix soonish (TODO, EB section) — re-draft this one line after the fix lands. Still DRAFT until the whole EB text is signed.

Stoneshaper imperative opener; missing cooldown

Terra Firma

"You animate earth to fight for you."

"Gaze at a creature, then at a wall, to carve an animate statue of that creature from the wall." "The statue inherits the creature's body plan and physique ({{rules|Strength}}, {{rules|Agility}}, {{rules|Toughness}}), but not its mutations, skills, or equipment…" "Statue hit points: {{rules|N}}" … [no Cooldown line]

Base touchstone — Stone Gaze (Lithofex)

"You turn things to stone with your gaze."

"You can gaze {{rules|N}} squares after a one-turn warmup and turn targets to stone. Cooldown: {{rules|N}} rounds"

  • ✓Flavor line is the best in the mod — seven words, perfect shape.
  • △"Gaze at a creature, then at a wall, to carve…" is imperative; base voice is "You gaze… / You may…" (see touchstone). One-clause fix.
  • △Rules-wrap on attribute names (Strength/Agility/Toughness) — base wraps values, not nouns.
  • ✗No Cooldown line despite a real cooldown — base lists it on essentially every activated power.
Suggested fix — DRAFT (de-imperative + split + cooldown; flavor line stands)

"You gaze first at a creature, then at a wall, and carve an animate statue of the creature from the wall. The statue inherits the creature's body plan and physique (Strength, Agility, Toughness). It does not inherit mutations, skills, or equipment. The statue fights with stone renditions of the creature's natural weapons. Nearby enemies must make a Willpower save (difficulty {{rules|N}}) or direct their attacks at the statue. Can target {{rules|stone}} walls Statue hit points: {{rules|N}} Statue AV: {{rules|N}} Statue attack: {{rules|N}} damage with {{rules|+N}} to-hit The statue lasts for {{rules|N}} rounds. Expired and released statues return to lifeless stone where they stand, entombing anything worn or carried. Cooldown: 120 rounds"

Imperative → "You gaze…" (Stone Gaze's own shape); the inheritance mega-line splits in three with attribute names unwrapped; Cooldown 120 (the code's BASE_COOLDOWN) lands last, bare per the flat-constant convention. The wall-type wrap stays — it scales with level, which is exactly what rules-wrap is for. The Lv10 "until destroyed" branch keeps its conditional shape unchanged.

Geomancy on-formula

Terra Firma

"You sense minute details through earthly vibrations."

"Stones you throw reveal the area around their point of impact." "Power equals the stone's {{rules|sell value}} or {{rules|weight}}, whichever is higher." "+{{rules|2}} thrown weapon range"

Base touchstone — Heightened Hearing

"You are possessed of unnaturally acute hearing."

"You detect the presence of creatures within a radius of {{rules|N}}." "Chance to identify nearby detected creatures"

  • ✓Passive-sense structure matches the Heightened family cleanly; "whichever is higher" has direct base precedent (Beguiling's success roll).
  • △Rules-wrap on words ("sell value", "weight", "full scanning") — base reserves the wrap for numbers. Unwrap and this is done.
Suggested fix — DRAFT, revised on recon at the 2026-07-26 sitting (structural)

"You sense minute details through earthly vibrations. Stones you throw reveal the area around their point of impact. Power equals the stone's sell value or weight, whichever is higher. Vision radius: 1 tile per 90 power (minimum 3) Vision duration: 1 round per 35 power (minimum 7) Gemstones also apply full scanning to creatures and walls in the revealed area. +2 thrown weapon range"

Recon found Geomancy is NON-LEVELED (CanLevel() => false) — and base's settled pattern for those (SlimeGlands, Frost Webs, Kindle, Telepathy) puts everything in the description after the flavor line and leaves level text empty, with NO rules-wraps at all: the wrap marks level-scaling values, and nothing here scales. So: mechanics move into GetDescription, GetLevelText returns "", every wrap goes bare — the original two word-unwraps are subsumed. Wording otherwise untouched. Build note: this is a GetDescription/GetLevelText restructure, not just string edits.

Fissure largest register drift — user call

Terra Firma

"You shake the stones of the deep ceiling loose and bring them down around you."

"The earth above you gives way, and its stones come down around you." "{{rules|N}} rocks fall within {{rules|R}} squares of you, crushing whatever they strike for their heft plus {{rules|L}}." "The ceiling nearest you falls thickest and hardest." "What falls lies where it fell: take it up and hurl it, or leave it underfoot." "Cooldown: {{rules|N}} rounds"

Base touchstone — Burgeoning

"You cause plants to spontaneously grow in a nearby area, hindering your enemies."

"Range: 8" "Area: 3x3 + growth into adjacent tiles" "Cooldown: {{rules|N}} rounds" "More powerful plants summoned"

  • ✓Cooldown last, rules-wrap correct, and the flavor line (though long at 15 words) is legal.
  • ✗The level text is a prose poem: its first line restates the flavor line (base never repeats), and "falls thickest and hardest" / "What falls lies where it fell:" are lyric register where base states facts.
  • ✗Mechanics are cast as flavor — "crushing … for their heft" instead of a "Damage:" label; the decay clock hides inside "Pocketed too long…".
  • △USER CALL: several of these lines document ruled mechanics ("Deeper strata shed stronger stone." shipped with the depth ruling). Conforming keeps every fact but restates them in base grammar; or rule the lyric register a deliberate mod signature and exempt Fissure. The mandate as ruled says conform.
Option A — DRAFT: conform (every ruled fact kept, poetry traded for grammar)

"{{rules|N}} rocks fall within {{rules|N}} squares of you, crushing what they strike. Damage: varies by stone, +{{rules|N}} Rocks fall more often and crush harder near you. Each collapse favors one seam of stone. About half the falls are that stone. The collapse sometimes spreads outward, and spreads farther at depth. Deeper strata shed stronger stone. Fallen stones persist and can be picked up and thrown. Enemies do not avoid fallen stones. Stones carried too long crumble to plain rock. Cooldown: {{rules|N}} rounds"

The duplicate opening line is gone (the flavor line already says it); "for their heft" becomes a Damage label; the semicolon in the seam line splits into two sentences. "Deeper strata shed stronger stone." survives verbatim — it was already base-shaped.

Option B — DRAFT: exempt the register, minimal touch

Keep the current text, minus its first line ("The earth above you gives way…" — it restates the flavor line, which base never does, whatever the register ruling says). Everything else stands as the mod's signature voice — but this needs the exemption RULED so the mandate's provenance stays clean.

Lithovore wry where base is precise — user call

Terra Firma

"You eat rocks. Only rocks."

"You can eat stones and gemstones; other food turns your stomach." "Some stones do you a bit of good on the way down. Others are inadvisable." "When you're famished, you may devour statues."

Base touchstone — Carnivorous

"You eat meat exclusively."

"You get no satiation from foods that aren't meat. If you eat raw food that isn't meat, there's a 50% chance you become ill for 2 hours. You can't cook with plant or fungus ingredients."

  • ✓Structure is exactly the defect pattern (flavor line, blank line, mechanic lines); "You can't cook with plant or meat ingredients." is near-verbatim Carnivorous grammar.
  • △"You eat rocks. Only rocks." — the fragment gag has no base precedent (Carnivorous: one flat sentence). It's also the mod's best-loved line. USER CALL.
  • ✗"Some stones do you a bit of good… Others are inadvisable." is coy where base quantifies ("50% chance… for 2 hours"). If the vagueness is deliberate discovery design, rule it; otherwise base wants the numbers.
Suggested fix — DRAFT (shared body; the flavor line is the ruling)

Body, either way: "You can eat stones and gemstones. You can't eat anything else. Many stones confer an effect when eaten. The dangerous ones warn you first. You can't cook with plant or meat ingredients. When you're famished, you may devour statues."

Flavor line: A) "You eat rocks exclusively." — the Carnivorous mirror, full conformance. B) "You eat rocks. Only rocks." — keep the gag, RULED as signature.

The body redraft fixes the real drift either way: "turns your stomach" becomes the flat refusal it actually is, and the coy danger line becomes the mechanically true fact — dangerous eats DO warn first (the ruled warn-prompt), so precision here costs no discovery. Per-stone numbers stay out: unlike Carnivorous's one 50% rule, Lithovore's effects are per-rock — that detail lives on the rocks, not the defect.

Automation verdict The question this page answers: can the mutation-description sitting be automated?

Yes — mechanically fixable (Claude drafts, low risk)

  • Add the two missing Cooldown lines (Calcify, Stoneshaper).
  • Split compound lines to fact-per-line (Gorgon's stone-mark line, EB's whorl block, Stoneshaper's inheritance line).
  • Unwrap non-numeric rules-wraps (sight, sell value, weight, full scanning, attribute names).
  • Delete or relocate editorial asides (EB's resonance parenthetical → the Attune ability's own description if wanted at all).
  • De-hedge Calcify's verb; convert Stoneshaper's imperative to "You gaze…" (one clause each).

No — needs a ruling first (register questions)

  • Fissure's lyric level text: conform to stat grammar (all facts kept) vs exempt as mod signature. The 2026-07-23 mandate says conform.
  • Lithovore's wryness: keep "Only rocks." and the coy danger line, or adopt Carnivorous-grade precision.
  • Flavor lines generally: they're identity work — automation can propose slates, but these seven sentences are where the user's voice matters most.

The drafts now live in each card's dashed block, so the sitting collapses to: rule the two register questions (Fissure A/B, Lithovore A/B, plus Calcify's flavor pick), veto or reword any draft lines, and Claude lands the signed-off set in the source in one build. The formula is regular enough that the checklist — not taste — decides most lines.
